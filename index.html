Calculation Formulas

Permeability of Free Space ($\mu_0$):

$$ \mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m} $$

B vs. H (in vacuum):

$$ H(\mathrm{A/m}) = \frac{B(\mathrm{T})}{\mu_0} \quad \Leftrightarrow \quad B(\mathrm{T}) = \mu_0\,H(\mathrm{A/m}) $$

Tesla vs. Gauss:

$$ 1\,\mathrm{T} = 10^4\,\mathrm{G} $$

A/m vs. Oersted:

$$ H(\mathrm{Oe}) = H(\mathrm{A/m}) \times \frac{4\pi}{10^3} \quad \Leftrightarrow \quad H(\mathrm{A/m}) = H(\mathrm{Oe}) \times \frac{10^3}{4\pi} $$
